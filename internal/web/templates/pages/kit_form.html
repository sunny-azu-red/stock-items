Warranty

Tick it for a job the vehicle's warranty asks for on time, like the oil service. While the warranty runs, a break-in or full service done more than 10% of its distance or 30 days late loses it, judged by this kit's distance and months as they are now. To change the job, edit its kit rather than adding a new one: a new kit has none of the old one's services, so every service due since the purchase counts as missed.

Parts

The parts the job needs, so you see at a glance what is on the shelf and what to buy. Quantities accept units, e.g. 1.7 L or 500 ml. Missing an item? Save the kit, add the item (with 0 in stock), then edit the kit to include it.
